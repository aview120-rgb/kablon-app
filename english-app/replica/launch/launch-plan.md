# Launch plan

1. **Closed test (required by Google for new personal accounts):** at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row
   before you can publish to production. Recruit family, friends, a WhatsApp group of parents / students.
2. Talk to the first 10 users by hand: what was hard, what voice/TTS worked on their phone.
3. Where learners are: Israeli Facebook groups for English learners & bagrut prep, r/Israel, r/hebrew, teachers' groups.
   Lead with the fix: "free, no daily limit, explanations in Hebrew".
4. Ask happy testers (never pay, never fake) to leave an honest Play review.
5. Track in Play Console: installs, crashes (Android vitals), ratings; AdMob: revenue, fill rate.
6. Release every 2–4 weeks: new units (B1), reminders, speaking practice.
