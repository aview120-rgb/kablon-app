Ad formats and expected earnings: banner (tab screens), interstitial (≤1 per 3 lessons), rewarded (opt-in, streak freeze).
Israeli eCPMs are low (roughly a few dollars per 1,000 impressions, varies a lot): expect pocket money,
not a salary, until there are thousands of daily users. Possible later: a one-time "remove ads" purchase.
