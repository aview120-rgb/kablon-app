## Palette
Primary violet `#5534d4` + warm orange accent `#ff8a3d` (category leaders use green / blue / orange-yellow). Contrast: 0 AA failures in light and dark (`replica/design/contrast.py`).

## Logo brief
Idea: a speech bubble holding an English "A" next to a Hebrew "א" – two languages in one conversation.
Mark: symbol + wordmark. Must read at 48px launcher size. Delivered: `public/icon.svg`, 1024 icon, adaptive Android icon, splash, Play feature graphic 1024×500 (`store/`).
Must not resemble any competitor mark: no animal mascot, no green.

## Voice: warm, short, encouraging
- warm, not childish · short, not cold · encouraging, not pushy
- Do: "מעולה!" / Don't: "WOW!!! אתה גאון!!!"
- Do: "לא בדיוק" + why / Don't: "טעות!" alone
- Do: "סיימו קודם את השיעור הקודם 🔒" / Don't: "שדרגו כדי לפתוח"
- Do: one emoji max per line / Don't: emoji rain
- Do: plural-neutral Hebrew (את/ה, סיימו) / Don't: male-only forms
