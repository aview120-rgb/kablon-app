## 2. What is missing
- Explanations of *why* an answer is wrong (grammar tips) – leaders show the right answer only. (recon sources)
- Speaking practice – [getfluently](https://getfluently.app/blog/best-english-learning-apps). *thin*

## 3. What is unsolved (positioning)
- **Hebrew speakers.** No leader targets Hebrew-specific errors (th/w/v, false friends like "sympathetic", word order)
  – [elitedor.dz](https://elitedor.dz/best-apps-to-learn-english-2026/) lists L1-targeted pronunciation for other languages only.

## The angle
1. **For Hebrew speakers who want to learn English without a daily limit or a paywall, Safa teaches in Hebrew, free, as much as you want.** ← *recommended* (themes 1 + unsolved)
2. For people tired of being nagged, Safa has no notifications and no ads inside lessons. (themes 2–3, thin)
